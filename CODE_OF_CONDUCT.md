# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in CamView to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent; critique ideas, not people.
- Keep discussions technical and on topic.
- Respect privacy: never post camera feeds, credentials, IP addresses or
  personal network details.
- Accept maintainer decisions gracefully and ask for clarification when needed.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or sexualized language.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Sharing camera credentials, recordings or personal data.
- Sustained disruption of issues, discussions or pull requests.

## Scope

This code applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages, documentation and any project-related communication.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through GitHub. The
maintainer may remove content, close discussions or block contributors as
appropriate. Reports will be handled as confidentially as possible.
